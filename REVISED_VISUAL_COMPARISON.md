## Implementation Complexity

All options require:
1. **Same config changes** in `cfg/base.yaml`
2. **Same file to modify**: `tpls/_common_05_daily.tpl`
3. **No changes** to `schedule.tpl` or other templates

**Difficulty:** Low — Single template file modification

## My Recommendation

### 🏆 **Option D: Compact Structured Debrief**

**Why this is the best choice:**

1. **Sweet spot for notes** — 15 lines is plenty for daily notes while leaving room for meaningful reflection

2. **Space-efficient debrief** — Side-by-side "Wins" and "Improve" makes great use of the full page width without taking too much vertical space

3. **Actionable structure** — The debrief focuses on concrete items:
   - What worked (to repeat)
   - What didn't (to improve)
   - What you learned (to remember)

4. **A5 optimized** — The proportions work especially well for smaller paper:
   - Schedule column: adequate for hour blocks
   - Right column: balanced between tasks and notes
   - Bottom section: compact but useful

5. **Not overwhelming** — Unlike Option A with 3 separate prompts, this is quick to fill out (2-3 minutes max)

6. **Daily rhythm** — Encourages a healthy practice of daily wins + improvements without feeling burdensome

## Alternative Recommendations

**If you want more notes space:** Choose **Option B** (18 lines + simple reflection)

**If you want maximum notes:** Choose **Option C** (20 lines + minimal reflection)

**If you like guided prompts:** Choose **Option A** (structured prompts)
